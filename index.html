    European dining is shaped by centuries of regional identity, seasonal terroir, and time-honored cooking traditions. From wood-fired Neapolitan dough and slow-simmered Spanish stews to crisp butter pastries in France and rustic mountain dishes across Central and Eastern Europe, every region tells a distinct culinary story. Here, travelers share the dishes, village taverns, and hidden eateries that made their trips unforgettable.

    Continental Bites is an ongoing culinary expedition across Europe’s vibrant terroir. From sun-ripened Mediterranean olive groves and alpine cheese cellars to centuries-old Nordic smokehouses, European gastronomy is shaped by regional tradition, seasonality, and time-honored craftsmanship. Whether exploring everyday rustic comfort foods or refined bistro classics, every plate carries a distinct sense of place.
